## Generated assets

These files are produced by scripts in this repository from public geodata
rather than drawn by hand. They are covered by the MIT licence above, but their
provenance matters if you regenerate them:

- **Terrain and city** — `assets/terrain/kigali_terrain.*` and
  `assets/terrain/kigali_city.*` are built by `tools/build_terrain.py` and
  `tools/build_city.py` from elevation and OpenStreetMap data for Kigali. The
  underlying OpenStreetMap data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under
  the [Open Database Licence](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/), and
  the elevation data comes from public SRTM releases. Attribution for those
  sources belongs with the derived meshes.
- **Sky, textures and audio** — `assets/sky/*`, the city texture set and
  `audio/*.wav` are generated by `tools/make_sky.py`, `tools/city_textures.py`
  and `tools/make_audio.py`, and are original output of this project.
